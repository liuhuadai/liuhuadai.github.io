\sectionhead{02}{Selected research agenda}
\agenda{01}{AudioCALM}{2026}{Continuous autoregressive modeling without discrete-token bottlenecks.}{Extends next-token prediction to continuous audio latents with a flow-matching head, block-causal AR-Flow, and asymmetric modality experts.}{One jointly trained model matches modality-specific state of the art across speech, sound, and music.}

\agenda{02}{STAR-VAE}{ICML · 2026}{Representation learning for continuous audio generation.}{Formalizes the Rate--Distortion--Regularity Trilemma and introduces channel-indexed topology-aware regularization with a CNN--Mamba backbone.}{State-of-the-art reconstruction and generation with stronger semantic preservation.}

\agenda{03}{PrismAudio}{ICLR · 2026}{Reasoning and reinforcement learning for video-to-audio.}{Decomposes semantic, temporal, aesthetic, and spatial reasoning into specialized chains of thought and rewards; Fast-GRPO makes multi-objective post-training practical.}{State of the art across all four dimensions and strong out-of-domain generalization.}

\agenda{04}{ThinkSound}{NeurIPS · 2025}{Reasoning before synthesis for interactive audio generation.}{A multimodal LLM produces AudioCoT plans for generation, object-centric refinement, and language-guided editing; a unified audio foundation model executes each stage.}{Open-source project lead; 1.4K+ GitHub stars and a complete training and inference release.}

\agenda{05}{FlashAudio \& AudioLCM}{ACL · 2024--25}{Moving audio generation from hundreds of steps to one.}{Rectified Flow and latent consistency objectives reshape generative trajectories for stable, high-fidelity few-step synthesis.}{400\texttimes{} and 333\texttimes{} real-time generation; FlashAudio received ACL Oral and SAC Highlight.}
