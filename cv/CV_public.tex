\cvsection{Research Profile}

Research spans speech, sound, music, and broader multimodal intelligence. \cvsection{Selected Research Highlights}
\researchitem{AudioCALM: Continuous Autoregressive Language Modeling for Universal Audio}{Preprint · 2026}{Replaces discrete-token prediction with flow matching over continuous audio latents; block-causal AR-Flow and asymmetric modality experts unify streaming speech, sound, and music generation in one autoregressive backbone.}

\researchitem{STAR-VAE: Structured Topology-Aware Regularization for Audio}{ICML · 2026}{Formalizes the Rate--Distortion--Regularity Trilemma and introduces channel-indexed regularization with a CNN--Mamba backbone, achieving state-of-the-art reconstruction and generation.}

\researchitem{PrismAudio: Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning for Video-to-Audio}{ICLR · 2026}{Decomposes semantic, temporal, aesthetic, and spatial reasoning into specialized chains of thought and rewards; Fast-GRPO makes multi-objective post-training practical.}

\researchitem{ThinkSound: Chain-of-Thought Reasoning for Audio Generation and Editing}{NeurIPS · 2025}{Introduces AudioCoT planning for any-to-audio generation, object-centric refinement, and language-guided editing in a unified open-source framework.}

\researchitem{FlashAudio: Rectified Flows for Ultra-Fast Text-to-Audio}{ACL Oral · SAC Highlight · 2025}{Introduces Rectified Flow for text-to-audio and enables single-step synthesis at \textbf{400$\times$ real time} while outperforming strong \mbox{diffusion} baselines.}

\researchitem{AudioLCM: Latent Consistency Models for Text-to-Audio}{ACM Multimedia · 2024}{Achieves high-quality generation in two to four steps at \textbf{333$\times$ real time}; the release became a widely used research baseline.}
